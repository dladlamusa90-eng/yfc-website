Ama2k Back2God: the night Witbank came home

1 October 20264 min readYFC Witbank

On Wednesday 23 September, Youth For Christ RSA and Peniel Community Church called the young people of Witbank to give God a whole night. They came. From eight in the evening until six the next morning the room worshipped, prayed and listened to the Word — and by the time the sun came up, young people had given their lives to Jesus and many had been baptised in the Holy Spirit. Glory to Jesus!

What
Called Out — Whole Night Prayer 2026, an all-night youth service themed Ama2k Back2God
When
Wednesday 23 September 2026, 20:00 until 06:00
Where
Peniel Community Church, behind Rondo Complex, Witbank
Who
Youth For Christ RSA with Peniel Community Church, and the young people of Witbank

Called out

“Ama2k” is what South Africa calls the generation born after 2000 — the young people who grew up with a phone in their hand and a hundred voices telling them who to be. Ama2k Back2God is a simple call to that generation: come back. Come back to the God who made you, knows you, and has not stopped looking for you.

The invitation Peniel Community Church sent out put it even more plainly. Called out from the kingdom of darkness into the kingdom of light. Come hungry, come ready, come expectant. Nobody was asked to be perfect before they walked in — only willing.

“But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God’s special possession, that you may declare the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his wonderful light.” 1 Peter 2:9

A whole night, given to God

There is something about staying up with God that you cannot get from an hour on a Sunday. There were loud hours: the praise team leading, the whole room singing, young people in front of the stage dancing like they meant every word. And there were quiet ones, when the worship turned into prayer that did not want to stop.

In between, the Word went out. One speaker after another stood up and preached to a generation that is told every day to be afraid — of failure, of the future, of what people will say. The Scripture on the screen answered all of it.

What God did

Then came the moment the whole night had been prayed towards. When the altar call was given, young people stood up and walked forward. Some came with their hands over their faces, some with a friend beside them — and there, in front of the pulpit, they confessed Jesus Christ as their Lord and Saviour.

And God did not stop there. As the ministry went on, many were baptised in the Holy Spirit. The same young people who arrived carrying the weight of the week left carrying something else entirely: the presence of God, and the knowledge that they are His.

We will not put a number on it. Every one of those decisions is a person with a name, a family and a story that has just started over, and heaven is rejoicing over each of them. Glory to Jesus!

Thank you, Peniel

A night like this does not happen by accident. Thank you to Peniel Community Church for opening your doors, for the refreshments, for arranging transport so that nobody had to stay home, and for believing with us that this generation is worth a sleepless night. And thank you to every young person who came, stayed and pressed in until morning.

If you gave your life to Christ that night

Welcome to the family. The decision you made is the beginning, not the end, and you do not have to walk it alone. Find a local church and plant yourself there. Get a Bible and start reading — the Gospel of John is a good place to begin. And join us online every Wednesday at 20:00, where young people from every branch open the Word together.

For everyone else: keep praying for the young people who said yes on the 23rd, that what God started in them would grow deep roots. Then watch the Events calendar — there are more nights like this coming, including the All Night Encounter in December.

The night was the start

Keep going with us: open the Bible every Wednesday, and be there for the next night we give to God.
