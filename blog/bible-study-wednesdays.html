Discipleship

Wednesdays at 20:00

17 September 20264 min read

Every Wednesday at 20:00 a link goes out and a room opens. An hour later it closes again. In between, young people from across our branches read the same passage and argue about what it actually means.

Platform
Google Meet
Day
Every Wednesday
Time
20:00 – 21:00
Bring
A Bible and a question

Why it is online

Our members are in Eastrand, Durban, Pretoria, Soweto, Potchefstroom, Gqeberha, Polokwane and Witbank. A Wednesday night that required a taxi would quietly exclude most of them, and the ones it would exclude first are the ones with the least money.

On Google Meet a student in Gqeberha and a member in Polokwane are in the same room at the same table. Nobody travels in the dark. Nobody misses out because the fare ran out on Tuesday.

What the hour looks like

It is deliberately short and deliberately plain. We open a passage and read it properly. We ask what it says before anyone asks what it means to me. Then we ask the harder question: what does this demand on Thursday morning, at work, at home, in the group chat.

An hour is not long enough to cover everything, which is the point. You leave with the passage still working on you instead of filed away as finished.

Bring the question you are embarrassed by

There is space to interrupt. That is not a lapse in the format, it is the format. We grow and encourage one another on purpose, and nobody grows by nodding along to something they did not follow.

If you have been a Christian for eleven years and still do not know how the Old Testament fits together, Wednesday is a good night to say so out loud.

The Daily Juice carries it through the week

Wednesday is one hour. The Daily Juice, our devotional book released weekly, covers the other six days, so the Word stays a rhythm rather than an appointment. Both sit alongside our other programs, and the Bible Study page has the joining details.

One thing this is not

It is not a replacement for your church. Our culture is explicit about it: every member belongs to a Bible-believing local church and serves there. Youth For Christ strengthens that commitment — we do not compete with it. You can read the rest of what we hold to on the YFC Culture page.

See you Wednesday

The link goes out on the WhatsApp channel every week. Bring a Bible.
