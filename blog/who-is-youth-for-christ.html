Who is Youth For Christ?

17 September 20266 min read

Youth For Christ is not a building and it is not a denomination. It is a group of young people from many different churches who decided that the Gospel is worth their weekends, their evenings and their ordinary conversations — and who keep showing up to prove it.

It started in Witbank

The movement is rooted in Witbank, Mpumalanga. What began there with a handful of young believers has grown into branches carrying the same mission into Eastrand, Durban, Pretoria, Soweto, Potchefstroom, Gqeberha, Polokwane and Witbank — one movement with many local expressions.

What holds it together is not a denomination. Our members come from many different churches, and that is on purpose. We are united by one passion: bringing the Gospel to people who do not yet know Christ, across denominational lines and cultural differences.

Why we go

Our mission comes straight out of Scripture, and we take it literally.

“Preach the word; be prepared in season and out of season; correct, rebuke, and encourage with great patience and careful instruction.” 2 Timothy 4:2

That is why you will find us doing street evangelism, door-to-door outreach, open-air crusades, schools ministry and campus ministry. It is also why we keep going back — because “in season and out of season” does not leave much room for only showing up when it is convenient.

Our vision follows 1 Timothy 2:4, that God desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth. And because there is one God and one Mediator between God and people, the man Christ Jesus, we carry this as a personal responsibility, not a hobby.

What happens through the week

Outreach is the visible part, but it sits on top of a weekly rhythm that most people never see:

  • The Daily Juice — our devotional book, released weekly, to keep members in the Word between gatherings.
  • Jesus Roundtable — a weekly panel discussion built around God’s Word, made especially for new believers finding their footing.
  • Weekly Bible study, prayer meetings and monthly fasting — the unglamorous engine room of everything else.
  • Youth For Christ Worship, the Atmosphere of Faith Tour and All Night Encounter — nights given to worship, prayer and encounter.
  • Leadership training, camp and the global leadership meeting — because young people are not only meant to be reached, they are meant to be raised.
  • CrossRoads, Comments Section, Q&A and sermons — our media platforms, where real questions get honest answers.

The culture underneath it

Every movement has a culture, whether it names it or not. We have named ours, and we hold each other to it.

  • Soul winning is a lifestyle, not an event. It does not clock out when the outreach ends.
  • We reason by the Word of God. Not by trends, not by feelings. Outside the Word we know nothing.
  • We honour and serve our local churches. Every member belongs to a Bible-believing local church and serves there. YFC does not replace the church; we strengthen commitment to it.
  • We grow and encourage one another. Nobody is left to go weak quietly.
  • We are family. We pray, serve, evangelise and stand together through every season.

Arise and shine

Our theme for 2026 is Arise and Shine, from Isaiah 60:1.

“Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.” Isaiah 60:1

It is a command with two halves. Arise is the part we do — get up, go out, stop waiting for a better feeling. Shine is the part that only happens because the light already came. We are not the source. We are just the ones who refuse to stay seated.

Where to start

If you are new here, the simplest entry points are a Bible study, the next thing on the events calendar, or an outreach with the branch closest to you. Read more about who we are and the culture we live by, and if you would rather just talk to a person, get in touch.

There is a place for you here, whether you are new to faith or you have been walking with Christ for years.
