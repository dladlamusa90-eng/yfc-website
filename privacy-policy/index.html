Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Youth For Christ International collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Introduction

Our Commitment to Privacy

Youth For Christ International ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Youth For Christ International website and related services. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your personal information.

By accessing or using our website and services, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our services.

Information We Collect

Types of Data

Contact Information: When you contact us via email, contact forms, or through our messaging systems, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and message content.

Event Registration: When you register for events or join our programs, we collect registration details including your name, email, contact information, and relevant event preferences.

Newsletter & Subscriptions: If you subscribe to our updates or join our WhatsApp channels, we collect your email or phone number.

Website Usage: We may collect information about how you interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral sources, through standard web analytics.

This data helps us improve our services and communicate effectively with you.

How We Use Your Information

Data Usage

We use your personal information to:

Communicate: Respond to your inquiries, send event updates, and share ministry news and announcements.

Provide Services: Process event registrations, manage program participation, and deliver the services you request.

Improve: Analyze website usage to improve our services, content, and user experience.

Legal Compliance: Fulfill legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We do not engage in data trading or marketing list sales.

Your trust is fundamental to our mission.

Data Protection & Security

Keeping Your Information Safe

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. This includes encryption for sensitive data and secure server infrastructure.

However, no method of internet transmission is entirely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You acknowledge this inherent risk when using our services.

Retention & Deletion

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Typically, we retain contact information for active community members and event data for archival purposes.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at youthsforchrist99@gmail.com. We will honor deletion requests within 30 days, except where we are required to retain information by law.

Your Rights

Your Privacy Controls

Access: You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

Correction: You can request that we update or correct inaccurate information.

Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

Opt-Out: You can unsubscribe from email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at youthsforchrist99@gmail.com with your request.

Third-Party Services

External Platforms

Our website may include links to external platforms and services, including Google Meet, WhatsApp, social media platforms, and Firebase-based services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing your information.

We use Firebase for secure data storage and event management. Firebase implements industry-standard security practices. For more information, please review Google's privacy policy.

Updates & Contact

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date at the bottom of this page. Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
