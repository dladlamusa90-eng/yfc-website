About YFC RSA

A national youth ministry rooted in the Gospel

Who We Are

Youth For Christ International (YFC RSA) began in Witbank, Mpumalanga, and now represents young people nationally through Christ-centered evangelism, discipleship, and outreach.

Origin and Reach

Origin: Witbank, Mpumalanga

Representation: National youth movement in South Africa

Mission

Preach the Word in season and out of season

Our mission follows 2 Timothy 4:2, calling this generation to bold evangelism, practical discipleship, and faithful service wherever God sends us.

2 Timothy 4:2

Vision

That all may be saved and come to the truth

Our vision reflects 1 Timothy 2:4, to raise young people who know Jesus, live in truth, and lead others into a life transformed by Christ.

1 Timothy 2:4

Organizational Structure

How ministry teams serve together

Evangelism

Leads outreach efforts, gospel campaigns, and one-on-one witnessing among youth and local communities.

Discipleship

Builds believers through Bible study, mentoring, prayer rhythms, and growth pathways for spiritual maturity.

Media

Shares testimonies, teachings, and ministry updates through digital platforms for wider impact and mobilization.

Worship Team

Creates an atmosphere of worship that leads young people into deeper intimacy with God and unified ministry gatherings.
